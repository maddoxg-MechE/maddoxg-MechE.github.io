# 🚁 Hybrid Aerial–Underwater Drone (Capstone Project)

## Project Overview
This capstone project focuses on the design of a hybrid aerial–underwater vehicle intended for coral reef research and marine ecosystem inspection. The system is being developed to operate seamlessly across air and water environments, enabling multi-domain data collection, improved accessibility to reef structures, and enhanced environmental monitoring capabilities.

Current work emphasizes early-stage systems engineering, including functional decomposition, component architecture development, and requirements definition for cross-medium transition, stability, and sensing.

## 🔧 Current Development Status
The project is currently in the conceptual and systems engineering phase. Active work includes defining requirements, decomposing system functions, and developing preliminary subsystem architecture to support dual-environment operation.

Future work will focus on trade studies, prototype design considerations, and validation of aerial–underwater transition feasibility.
