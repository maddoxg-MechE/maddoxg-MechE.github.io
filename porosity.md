# Investigating Porosity in 3D Printed PLA

## Project Overview

This project investigates how nozzle temperature affects porosity in PLA parts produced by 3D printing. Porosity can reduce mechanical strength, thermal performance, and part reliability. Understanding the relationship between nozzle temperature and pore formation is essential for optimizing print quality.

The goal of this study is to quantify how different nozzle temperatures influence pore size, distribution, and total porosity in PLA cubes. The results will help identify optimal printing conditions to minimize defects.

## Methodology

The project will follow a systematic experimental approach:

- **Printing:** Five PLA cubes will be printed at different nozzle temperatures.  
- **Slicing:** Each cube will be sliced into five layers for detailed analysis.  
- **Imaging:** Each layer will be photographed under a microscope to capture pore structure.  
- **Image Analysis:** The layer images will be processed using Fiji (ImageJ) to combine them and quantify porosity. Porosity will be calculated as the ratio of void area to total area for each cube.  
- **Comparison:** Results will be compiled across all temperatures to evaluate trends and identify the optimal nozzle setting for minimizing pores.

By carefully analyzing how nozzle temperature affects porosity, this project aims to provide actionable guidelines for producing stronger, more consistent 3D printed PLA parts.

## Objectives

- Quantify porosity percentage for PLA cubes printed at different nozzle temperatures  
- Analyze pore size, distribution, and connectivity across layers  
- Determine the optimal nozzle temperature to minimize defects and improve part reliability
